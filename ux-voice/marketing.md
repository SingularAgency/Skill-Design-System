# Marketing voice & tone

**Use for:** Website, presentations, proposals, case studies, social, marketing
email, and public Design System narrative

**Owner:** Product Design; reviewed by Marketing

Marketing helps the right buyer recognize an operating problem, understand
Singular's approach, distinguish proof from promise, and choose a useful next
step. It applies [Client reality](../docs/01-client-context.md) and
[Brand & core voice](../brand/README.md) without redefining them.

Good Singular marketing makes a founder think, “Yes—that is exactly what keeps
coming back to me,” before asking them to care about our model.

## The reader and the decision

Write primarily for a founder, owner, executive operator, or Business Champion
inside an established US SMB.

They are not deciding whether AI is interesting. They are deciding whether one
important workflow can create leverage without reducing control, disrupting the
team, or creating another dependency.

Marketing must answer:

1. Do you understand the operating problem?
2. What changes, and why is Singular the right partner?
3. What is proven, provisional, or unknown?
4. What is the next commitment?

Strong fit requires a consequential recurring workflow, an accountable owner,
a visible consequence, willingness to change how work happens, and a way to
evaluate the result. Fit is weak when the request is only for a demo, no one
owns the workflow, or success cannot be defined.

## Positioning

Singular is an outcome partner that turns one consequential workflow into a
governed operating capability the client can own and adopt.

Use these boundaries:

- start with operating pain, not an AI category;
- describe changed work, not disconnected tools or automations;
- include context, ownership, review, and adoption in the mechanism;
- make client ownership and meaningful human judgment explicit.

Avoid positioning Singular as an AI consultancy, tool shop, chatbot vendor, or
partner that owns the client's systems.

Company Brain, Rivers, and Totems are optional explanations:

- **Rivers:** changing operating signals.
- **Totems:** stable rules, permissions, definitions, and institutional
  knowledge.
- **Company Brain:** context that lets people and agents interpret live signals
  through stable rules.

Define the operating idea before the metaphor. Do not use these terms when they
add another concept the reader must learn.

## Narrative

Use this sequence when it helps the buyer decide:

```text
operating future or recognizable pain
→ business consequence
→ why the problem persists
→ changed operating model
→ Singular's role
→ evidence or constraint
→ next step
```

The hero may lead with the desired future. The body must prove that Singular
understands the obstacle before introducing Company Brain, agents, platforms,
or tools.

### The hero in practice

**Hero**

> Turn the business you're running into the business that runs itself.

**Support**

> Growth has spread context and decisions across people and tools. Singular
> turns one consequential workflow into a governed capability your team can
> understand, use, and own.

The hero is intentionally ambitious. The support gives it an operating
boundary. Do not add a number merely to make it sound concrete.

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Calm, declarative, ambitious | Make the operating future visible |
| Problem | Specific, empathetic | Create recognition without dramatizing |
| Mechanism | Precise, plain | Explain how work changes |
| Outcome | Concrete, evidence-led | Show what becomes different |
| Proof | Factual, restrained | Support trust |
| CTA | Direct, low-friction | Name the next step |
| Presentation | Executive, structured | Move from context to decision |
| Social or email | Immediate, human | Earn attention without clickbait |

## Hear the difference

The fastest way to learn the voice is to hear the same idea said two ways.
“Not Singular” may be grammatically correct, but it hides the operating reality,
performs certainty, or could belong to any AI company. The Singular version
sounds like someone who understands the work and respects the reader.

These examples are illustrative patterns, not approved customer facts or
commercial claims.

### Recognizing the problem

| Situation | Not Singular | Singular |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | “Scale smarter with AI.” | “Growth should not send every important decision back to the founder.” |
| Fragmented context | “Unify your data for better insights.” | “Your team already has the context. It is scattered across people and tools when the decision arrives.” |
| Manual coordination | “Streamline your operations.” | “If every handoff needs a Slack message, the workflow is not doing enough.” |
| Reporting | “Automate reporting and save time.” | “Senior operators should not spend Monday rebuilding numbers the business already has.” |
| Key-person dependency | “Reduce operational risk.” | “The workflow should carry the rules your best operator currently keeps in their head.” |
| AI pilot | “Unlock the full potential of AI.” | “A chatbot that never changes weekly work is still another tool to manage.” |
| Adoption | “Enjoy seamless adoption.” | “If the team cannot see, correct, or stop it, they will work around it.” |
| Efficiency | “Do more with less.” | “Give the team a dependable workflow without asking them to carry the system in their heads.” |

### Explaining Singular

| Situation | Not Singular | Singular |
|---|---|---|
| Category | “We are an AI transformation consultancy.” | “We turn one consequential workflow into a capability your team can run and own.” |
| Starting point | “We identify high-impact AI use cases.” | “We start where lost context and repeated handoffs are already costing the business.” |
| Mechanism | “Our agents automate end-to-end processes.” | “We connect the context, rules, owners, and decisions required to make one workflow dependable.” |
| Role of agents | “Deploy an autonomous AI workforce.” | “Agents handle bounded work. People keep the decisions where judgment matters.” |
| Existing tools | “Replace your fragmented tech stack.” | “We make the systems you already rely on work as one governed workflow.” |
| Client ownership | “We own the systems so you can run the business.” | “Your team owns the workflow, assets, and decision history we create together.” |
| Partnership | “Our experts deliver a custom solution.” | “Your team brings operating context and decisions. Singular brings system design, implementation judgment, and evidence discipline.” |
| Company Brain | “Build an intelligent Company Brain.” | “A Company Brain connects live operating signals with the rules and context people need to interpret them.” |

### Describing outcomes and evidence

Use a number only when its source, scope, comparison, period, and publication
permission are recorded.

| Situation | Not Singular | Singular |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | “Go live in 10 days.” | “After the workflow audit, we document the scope, dependencies, and dated delivery plan.” |
| Savings | “Cut operating costs by 90%.” | “In [workflow], weekly coordination fell from [baseline] to [result] over [period]. Source: [approved study].” |
| Faster work | “Move faster with intelligent automation.” | “Renewal risk reaches the account owner within [verified time], instead of waiting for the weekly report.” |
| Scale | “A scalable AI-native solution.” | “New locations inherit the same approved rules, permissions, and exception path.” |
| Delivery | “We delivered a successful automation.” | “The workflow passed QA and entered client review. Business impact is still being measured.” |
| ROI | “Guaranteed ROI from day one.” | “We will agree on the baseline, cost, and outcome measure before implementation begins.” |
| Customer proof | “Customers love the results.” | “[Approved verbatim quote].” — [Name, role, company] |
| Uncertainty | “This will transform the business.” | “This workflow is a strong candidate. The audit still needs to confirm data access, exceptions, and ownership.” |

### Headlines and supporting copy

A headline carries one idea in the buyer's operating language. Supporting copy
adds the audience, mechanism, or boundary instead of repeating it.

| Placement | Not Singular | Singular |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage hero | “Unlock transformative AI-powered growth.” | “Build a business that moves without sending every decision back to you.” |
| Hero support | “Our innovative solutions help businesses scale.” | “Start with one workflow where context is scattered, handoffs repeat, and one owner can measure the result.” |
| Problem section | “The future of work is here.” | “Your best people should not be the integration layer.” |
| Problem support | “Legacy systems slow down innovation.” | “Context is split across tools, while decisions keep returning to the same few people.” |
| Mechanism section | “AI that works for you.” | “Connect the signals that change with the rules that should not.” |
| Case study | “How AI transformed a growing business.” | “How one renewal workflow stopped depending on a Monday spreadsheet.” |
| Design System | “A unified digital experience.” | “One identity. Every surface. The same logic from story to state.” |

Contrast—“Not X. Y.”—is optional. Use it to correct a false category, not as a
signature in every section.

### Calls to action

Name the actual next step and verify that the commercial path exists before
publishing it.

| Intent | Not Singular | Singular |
|---|---|---|
| Explore the model | “Learn more” | “See how the model works” |
| Qualify the opportunity | “Get started” | “Map the first workflow” |
| Begin discovery | “Unlock your AI potential” | “Start the AI audit” |
| Speak with Singular | “Contact us” | “Talk through the bottleneck” |
| Schedule | “Book now” | “Book a strategy call” |
| Read proof | “Discover more” | “Read the case study” |
| Send a form | “Submit” | “Send the workflow brief” |

### Presentations and proposals

Use one decision or claim per slide. The title states the takeaway; the body
separates current state, proposed change, evidence, risk, and decision.

| Slide job | Not Singular | Singular |
|---|---|---|
| Problem | “Automation opportunities” | “Manual follow-up is delaying renewals and hiding account risk.” |
| Current state | “Current workflow” | “Four systems and two handoffs stand between a risk signal and the account owner.” |
| Root cause | “Key challenges” | “The report is late because context has to be rebuilt before every review.” |
| Proposal | “Our AI solution” | “Route renewal risk with the account context, rule, owner, and review path attached.” |
| Human control | “Human in the loop” | “The account owner reviews exceptions before any customer-facing action.” |
| Evidence | “Expected impact” | “The workflow can reduce coordination; the baseline and target still require approval.” |
| Risk | “Dependencies” | “CRM access is the only unresolved dependency before implementation.” |
| Decision | “Next steps” | “Decide whether to authorize the workflow audit.” |

### Social and email

Be immediate and human without manufacturing urgency or enthusiasm.

| Situation | Not Singular | Singular |
|---|---|---|
| Social hook | “AI is changing everything. Don't get left behind.” | “Your best operator should not have to explain the same workflow every Monday.” |
| Point of view | “Businesses must embrace AI to stay competitive.” | “Another AI tool is not leverage if the team still rebuilds the context by hand.” |
| Announcement | “We're thrilled to unveil our revolutionary solution!” | “We built a clearer way to review scope, evidence, and the next decision.” |
| Outreach | “Hope this email finds you well. We help companies transform with AI.” | “You mentioned renewal reporting still depends on two senior operators. That is the workflow I would start with.” |
| Follow-up | “Just bumping this to the top of your inbox.” | “The open question is whether the renewal workflow has one accountable owner. If it does, we can map the next step.” |
| Progress update | “Our team is working hard behind the scenes.” | “We mapped the handoffs slowing the workflow. Two data-access questions remain.” |
| Urgency | “Last chance to transform your business.” | “If this workflow is still delaying renewals, let's decide whether it is worth auditing now.” |
| Close | “Let us know if you have any questions!” | “Reply with the workflow you would fix first.” |

## Claims and evidence

Use the canonical [evidence appendix](../docs/08-application-map.md#evidence-appendix)
before publishing a metric, speed, pricing, ROI, testimonial, logo, or customer
result. Never invent or reconstruct proof. Terms such as scalable, faster,
intelligent, AI-native, or automation require a defined behavior or comparison.

## Marketing review

- Are the buyer, problem, and consequence recognizable before the tool?
- Does the mechanism explain changed work, ownership, and review?
- Are the CTA and evidence boundary explicit?
- Is the language calm, specific, and unmistakably Singular?
